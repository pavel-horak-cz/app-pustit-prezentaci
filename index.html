Keyboard backup

Enter starts the presentation
Arrows turn pages
Backspace ends the presentation
M (hold) plays music
U turns the pointer on and off
K hides and shows the camera preview
F full screen

Hidden feature

Heart (both hands, made of two index fingers and two thumbs) plays music for as long as you hold it
