SlidesByHand / Clicker alternative

A presentation clicker alternative that needs no hardware

Open the app

A presentation clicker is a small remote that turns your slides. It works well, until you forget it at home, its battery dies or its USB receiver does not fit the laptop you were handed. SlidesByHand is an alternative that needs no hardware at all: your hand is the remote and the laptop camera is the receiver.

When a clicker is the better choice

If you walk around a large stage, present in a dark hall or depend on animations and videos inside PowerPoint, keep the clicker. It reaches further, does not care about light and controls the original presentation file.

When SlidesByHand is the better choice

  • Meetups and small rooms, where you stand next to the laptop anyway.
  • Several speakers in a row. Nobody passes a clicker around, and nobody hunts for a window between talks.
  • A borrowed or locked-down computer, where you cannot plug in or install anything.
  • The day you forgot the clicker. Open the page and carry on.
  • When you want the room to pay attention. Starting your slides with an open palm is a small moment people remember.

How it compares to gesture scripts for programmers

If you search for gesture control of presentations, most of what you find are tutorials and Python scripts built on OpenCV. They are good for learning, but you have to install Python and several libraries, run the script from a command line and keep it running next to your presentation. SlidesByHand does the same job as one web page. There is nothing to install and it works the same on every laptop with a browser and a camera.
